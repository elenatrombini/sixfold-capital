# Sixfold Capital

**Website: [sixfold-capital.github.io](https://sixfold-capital.github.io/)**

Sixfold Capital is the fund created by our team of six for the Asset Management Challenge 2026 at USI Lugano. We combine macroeconomic research with quantitative analysis to build a global multi-asset portfolio.

The website presents the fund, our investment approach and the team, and hosts our research reports and performance updates for each round of the challenge. It is available in English, Italian and French.

## Pages

- **Home**: about the fund, our philosophy, approach and team
- **Investment Approach**: from economic outlook to portfolio decisions
- **Research & Reports**: reports and presentations for each round
- **Performance**: portfolio holdings and performance updates
- **Contact**
